## Decision 3 — model-unselected sides (ALLOW ONLY EXPLICIT, LABELED MATHEMATICAL COMPLEMENTS; NO NEW MODEL SIGNAL)

Export **all quoted market sides**, not just model-picked sides. For an exhaustive, correctly oriented binary market without pushes, `P(opposite)=1-P(selected)` is a valid **derived** complement if the underlying estimate is actually a probability of that complete event. With pushes, the exact opposite-win probability is `1-P(selected_win)-P(push)`; if the model only supplies conditional decisive P, the conditional complement is `1-P(selected_win | decisive)` and must be labelled accordingly.

A model-specific calibrated prediction for **only a selected signal/regime** is **not** proof that the opposite side has a calibrated betting edge. For unselected sides, set `model_signal_status=NOT_EVALUATED_BY_MODEL` and `original_tier=NONE/NOT_ASSIGNED`; show a derived conditional complement in a separate field only when mathematically justified, with `probability_source=DERIVED_COMPLEMENT`. Otherwise use `null` and an explicit reason. Never call the unselected side `Pass` just because the model didn't evaluate it. Never issue tiers/recommendations from complement-only display.

**Acceptance:** all six sides for ordinary games (offered vs absent/suspended explicit) with provenance, status and no fabricated edges.

## Decision 4 — NCAAFB push-excluded probability (MAKE IT EXPLICITLY CONDITIONAL)

The Week8+ PP V2 and continuous V1 spread probabilities are documented conditional on a **decisive/nonpush** result. Store `p_win_given_decisive` and `p_loss_given_decisive=1-p`; `p_push=null` and `push_probability_status=UNKNOWN` on eligible integer lines unless a frozen independent push estimator exists. Do NOT populate `p_push=0` for integer lines or misrepresent decisive P as unconditional P(win).

For half-point spread lines where pushes are mathematically impossible under applicable settlement rules, `p_push=0` can be explicit. With quoted net-profit multiple `b` and independent refunded push probability `q`, the unconditional per-unit EV is `(1-q) * (b * p_win_given_decisive - (1-p_win_given_decisive))`. If `q` is unknown, show **conditional-decisive EV** separately and leave unconditional EV unknown (though the sign is unchanged when q<1). The stand-alone Kelly maximizer for a refunded push, if the conditional probability is valid and applicable, equals the decisive-only Kelly fraction; label it as a **conditional single-bet diagnostic**, not a certified joint portfolio size.

**Adapter interface v2:** must support `CONDITIONAL_SCORED` / conditional probabilities or an equivalent separate conditional forecast record, because current `ScoreRow` requires three probabilities summing to 1. Preserve historical quote-to-score records and mark missing push estimate, not `PASS`.
